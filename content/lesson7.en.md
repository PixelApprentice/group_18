# Advanced Concepts — Practical Summary (Beginner)

This summary brings together important ideas you have already seen and shows practical next steps to keep improving a small project.

Topics and quick actions:
- See `content/core_tips.en.md` for a short checklist covering SQLi, XSS, CSRF, authentication, HTTPS, and logging.

Quick practical checklist before deploying a small app:
- Follow the core tips and apply one small improvement before deploying (e.g., enable HTTPS, enable HSTS, fix one dependency vulnerability).

Further practice links:
- OWASP Top Ten: https://owasp.org/www-project-top-ten/
- PortSwigger practical labs: https://portswigger.net/web-security

You are now ready to try the final quizzes and practice labs. Good job!

Progression — what to try next

Level 1 — Beginner
- Follow the core checklist and apply one small change.

Level 2 — Intermediate
- Perform a dependency scan, fix one vulnerability, and test your app.

Level 3 — Advanced (fun)
- Run a local lab (PortSwigger or TryHackMe) and document the exploit and the fix you applied.
